Get to know the person behind E-TECH CREATIVES.

Our founder

Engineer Edward Salonik

Founder & Software Engineer — E-TECH CREATIVES

E-TECH CREATIVES was founded by Engineer Edward Salonik, a software engineer with a strong passion for technology, creativity, and building practical digital solutions.

The journey of E-TECH CREATIVES began while Edward was still pursuing his Bachelor’s Degree in Software Engineering. What started as a university-driven passion for web development, software engineering, and digital design gradually grew into a vision: to build a technology company capable of helping businesses and individuals turn ideas into real, functional, and impactful digital products.

Rather than waiting until graduation to begin his professional journey, Edward started working on real-world projects, gaining hands-on experience in full-stack web development, software development, UI/UX, cybersecurity, and graphic design. Working directly with clients helped shape his understanding of an important principle: technology should not only look impressive — it should solve real problems, improve business operations, strengthen brands, and create opportunities for growth.

That philosophy became the foundation of E-TECH CREATIVES.

Today, E-TECH CREATIVES works with businesses, startups, organizations, entrepreneurs, and personal brands, helping them establish and strengthen their digital presence through professionally developed websites, software systems, creative design, and other digital solutions.

Under Edward’s leadership, the company continues to grow around a simple mission: to combine technology and creativity to build solutions that are modern, practical, reliable, and tailored to each client’s goals.

From developing business websites and digital platforms to creating custom software solutions and visual brand experiences, Edward remains actively involved in transforming client ideas from concepts into working solutions.

For him, E-TECH CREATIVES is more than just a technology company. It is the result of a journey that began with curiosity, learning, and ambition at university — and continues with a commitment to innovation, quality, creativity, and helping businesses succeed in an increasingly digital world.
